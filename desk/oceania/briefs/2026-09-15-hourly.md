## 1. Priority developments (ranked)

### 1. Marshall Islands — first Pacific WIM ExCom (Loss & Damage)
- **What happened:** **FACT.** The 25th meeting of the Executive Committee of the Warsaw International Mechanism is underway in Majuro, 14–18 Sep — the first time the global Loss & Damage body has sat in the Pacific ([PINA, 15 Sep](https://pina.com.fj/2026/09/15/wim-executive-committee-on-loss-and-damage-meets-in-pacific-for-first-time/); [PINA, 14 Sep](https://pina.com.fj/2026/09/14/pacific-frontline-nations-demand-action-as-loss-and-damage-talks-open-in-majuro/)). RMI Minister-in-Assistance Jess Gasper framed the venue as lived exposure (ocean proximity; Bikini community displacement), not a paper exercise. PINA attributes the Pacific sitting to the **COP31 Australia–Pacific Partnership**. Agenda items reported by the Loss & Damage Collaboration (advocacy group, **CLAIM** on priorities): voluntary L&D data guidelines; Action and Support Expert Group; science including Indigenous knowledge; terms of reference for a State of Loss and Damage Report; coordination with the Santiago Network and the Fund for Responding to Loss and Damage.
- **Why it matters for Oceania:** Climate is core security. A COFA state is hosting the UN L&D machinery in the run-up to Pacific Pre-COP and COP31. That is PIC agency and Forum climate diplomacy, not a US–China access story. It also fills a collection gap in this morning’s daily (MHL was marked quiet).
- **Actors & geography:** MHL (Majuro); WIM ExCom; AUS (COP31 partnership); UNFCCC architecture.
- **Confidence / sourcing:** H for venue/dates/first-Pacific (Tier B PINA, multi-day). M for “enabled by COP31 partnership” (PINA attribution, not a published AUS joint communiqué in this scan). L–M for L&DC five-point ask (advocacy, not ExCom decisions).
- **Watch next:** any ExCom decision text 18 Sep; AUS/DFAT confirmation of the partnership role; whether FRLD/Santiago Network operational language tightens.

### 2. Fiji — FiTI first-mover + MSME parametric insurance
- **What happened:** **FACT.** Fisheries Minister Alitia Bainivalu signed an MoU with FiTI Executive Director Sven Biermann in Rome at FAO COFI37. Fiji is the first Pacific Island country to commit to implementing the FiTI Standard; Bainivalu tied it to stock assessment, **fairer access agreements**, IUU, and public catch/permit data ([PINA](https://pina.com.fj/2026/09/15/fiji-commits-to-greater-transparency-in-fisheries-sector/)). **FACT.** Tower Insurance Fiji launched Fiji’s first climate-risk **parametric** product for MSMEs, locally underwritten, with UNCDF/ADB and PICAP (UNCDF, UNDP Pacific, UNU-EHS); PICAP support includes AUS and NZ MFAT. Design used a survey of 851 MSMEs via the Ministry of Commerce; RBF and government were involved ([PINA](https://pina.com.fj/2026/09/15/fiji-launches-first-climate-risk-insurance-for-small-businesses/)).
- **Why it matters for Oceania:** Observable mechanisms: a transparency MoU that can change bargaining over tuna access, and a payout-on-trigger insurance instrument that is adaptation finance in commercial clothing. Neither is a security pact. FiTI is a potential template other PICs can copy or resist.
- **Actors & geography:** FJI; FiTI; FAO; Tower; UNCDF/ADB/UNDP; AUS; NZL.
- **Confidence / sourcing:** H for both launches as reported (Tier B PINA; named ministers/firms). Amounts and trigger thresholds **not stated**.
- **Watch next:** FiTI implementation timetable / first public dataset; parametric trigger definitions and take-up; any PALM/RSE health-screening delta on the HIV emergency (still no gazette in this window).

### 3. Vanuatu — legal track hardens; French Pacific SAR assist
- **What happened:** Toll band **unchanged** vs the 07:00 UTC daily: 2 confirmed dead, 14 survivors, ~30+ missing; 10 vessels still searching east of Santo ([VBTC](https://vbtc.vu/10-vessels-continue-mv-matui-search/); [RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/1393319/vanuatu-pm-scraps-unga-trip-as-dozens-remain-missing-after-ferry-disaster)). **FACT (named official).** VMSA Deputy Commissioner Lloyd Fikiasi told the Vanuatu Daily Post the vessel was not certified for more than 30 passengers, left Luganville without VMSA knowledge, skipped a mandatory pre-departure inspection despite four VMSA safety staff on Santo, and that master/owners **could face manslaughter charges**. Safety kit including EPIRB was adequate for the certified load; VMSA will investigate why the beacon was not activated. Tui’s Shipping had not commented. AMSA supplied a generic Australian domestic-vessel duties explainer — **not** an AMSA investigation ([Daily Post](https://www.dailypost.vu/news/mv-matui-not-certified-to-carry-more-than-30-passengers-bypassed-mandatory-pre-departure-inspections/article_38e0ede1-9c58-5230-a6fa-82410a3fdbcd.html)). **CLAIM (AP citing NCL JRCC Facebook):** New Caledonia’s maritime SAR agency is coordinating an air and sea search and gave missing as **37** ([AP via WRAL](https://www.wral.com/news/ap/7aa92-questions-grow-after-vanuatu-ferry-sinking-as-rescuers-search-for-more-than-30-missing/)).
- **Why it matters for Oceania:** Governance test, not a great-power story. French Pacific capacity is now in the SAR picture (the morning daily was watching for NZ/AUS assist). Manslaughter language, if it proceeds, is a domestic accountability signal before cyclone season. Still **not** SOP §8 FLASH: no confirmed state-capacity breach, no capital unrest, no Category-level hydro-met disaster.
- **Actors & geography:** VUT (Santo/Sanma–Ambae); VMSA; PMO; NCL JRCC; AMSA (comment only).
- **Confidence / sourcing:** H for Fikiasi/SITREP legal track (Tier B named official). M for NCL coordination (AP; primary is Facebook). L–M for missing=37 vs VUT “30+” / families “~50”.
- **Watch next:** passenger manifest; whether manslaughter file is opened; NCL/VUT joint SITREP; any NZ/AUS asset offer.
