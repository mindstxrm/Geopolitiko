### 1. Vanuatu — MV Matui SAR continues; PM scraps UNGA; VMSA pulled under PMO
- **What happened:** **FACT.** Inter-island ferry MV Matui sank Friday 11 Sep ~10:00 between Santo and Ambae. As of Tuesday evening NZT, VMSA reported 14 survivors recovered and two confirmed deaths; ~30 or more remain missing ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/1393319/vanuatu-pm-scraps-unga-trip-as-dozens-remain-missing-after-ferry-disaster); [VBTC](https://vbtc.vu/10-vessels-continue-mv-matui-search/)). Napat cancelled the New York UNGA delegation “to attend to urgent domestic matters” and will fly to Santo. **FACT:** PMO has taken oversight of VMSA from the Ministry of Ocean and Maritime Affairs. **CLAIM (Vanuatu Daily Post via RNZ):** vessel deregistered in June and not certified for >30 passengers. **CLAIM (VMSA official to RNZ):** shipping-agent headcount was “less than 10”; families indicate ~50 missing. Marine warnings for 21–30 kt winds / ~3 m seas had been in force; police were not told the vessel failed to arrive until Saturday morning ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/1367205/vanuatu-ferry-disaster-exposes-gap-between-warnings-and-action); VBTC).
- **Why it matters for Oceania:** Climate-security is operational here: warning issuance ≠ maritime enforcement. A PIC leader skipping UNGA (climate, L&D, 1.5°C diplomacy) to run domestic SAR is a sovereignty/capacity signal, not a great-power story. Regulatory failure, if confirmed, is a governance test ahead of cyclone season.
- **Actors & geography:** VUT (Santo/Sanma–Ambae); PMO; VMSA; NDMO Luganville; UNGA-81.
- **Confidence / sourcing:** H for cancellation, deaths/survivors band, and PMO–VMSA shift (Tier A/B: PMO via RNZ, VMSA, VBTC). L–M for onboard total and deregistration (disputed / single-outlet).
- **Watch next:** official passenger manifest; investigation ToR; any NZ/AUS SAR-assist offer; whether UNGA statement is delivered in absentia.

### 2. Bougainville — Panguna camp attack; Toroama tells investors work continues
- **What happened:** **FACT.** Armed men entered a Lloyds Panguna Metals & Energy camp at the B20/Panguna site early Sunday 13 Sep and burned contractor equipment (Thrivenny). President Toroama (15 Sep) called it an isolated act, said Lloyds remains committed, and tasked Bougainville Police to protect personnel and assets ([PINA](https://pina.com.fj/2026/09/15/bougainville-president-toroama-assures-investors-after-panguna-camp-violence/); [PMN](https://pmn.co.nz/read/pacific-region/panguna-mine-attack-puts-bougainville-s-redevelopment-focus-under-fresh-pressure)). Panguna Landowners Interim Council apologised to Lloyds and restated support for legal/customary redevelopment (PMN). Marape urged calm (PMN). **CLAIM (OCCRP 14 Sep):** two people shot in a subsequent exchange; seven arrested — not independently confirmed in PINA/PMN copy ([OCCRP](https://www.occrp.org/en/news/two-shot-several-arrested-in-clashes-over-papua-new-guinea-mine)).
- **Why it matters for Oceania:** Panguna is the ABG’s fiscal theory of independence. Violence at the Indian-investor camp tests whether Bougainville can convert the 2019 referendum (results tabled in PNG parliament last month — OCCRP context) into a bankable state-building path without a return to conflict.
- **Actors & geography:** PNG / Autonomous Region of Bougainville (Panguna); ABG; BPS; Lloyds Metals (IND); landowners council.
- **Confidence / sourcing:** H for arson + Toroama statement (Tier B multi-outlet). M for arrests. L for casualties until BPS/hospital confirmation.
- **Watch next:** BPS incident report; whether feasibility work actually restarts on site; Port Moresby vs Buka sequencing on independence ratification.

### 4. Fiji — HIV epidemic declared a national emergency
- **What happened:** **FACT.** On 15 Sep Health Minister Lalabalavu said an outbreak-level response is no longer enough and declared a national emergency: estimated **1 in 60** adults living with HIV vs 1 in 167 five years ago; expand testing, ART, PrEP; mobilise a needle-and-syringe programme; five-member Cabinet sub-committee ([FBC](https://www.fbcnews.com.fj/news/hiv-declared-a-national-emergency/)).
